## Scope
Whole app (index.html shell + all views). Mode: Operate (learning tool), expression lives in color, shape, motion.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Verbträning is a street of painted Swedish timber houses. Each tense, card type and section is its own facade colour, so colour carries meaning (infinitiv sage, presens butter, preteritum rose, supinum sky) instead of decorating. Refuses the cold white-card-plus-blue-header utility look.
OWN-WORLD: Pastel facade paints (sage, butter, rose, sky, lilac, peach, mint) on a pale cool ground, one confident indigo ink/primary for text and actions. Shapes from architecture: arched door tops, round oriel windows, gabled skyline strip, pill chips. Friendly sans (Familjen Grotesk display, Figtree body). Soft tactile shadows, no outlines. Dark mode = night street: deep indigo ground with muted, lower-lightness paints.
STORY: A learner opens it and feels welcomed ("Hej!"), sees exactly one next step as a big arched door, and each verb form has a colour they learn to recognise.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sky-blue header band with sun and a skyline of 8 pastel houses standing on the page edge; nav as four differently coloured pill tabs. Below: "Hej!" greeting, then the next-step card as an arched door in a facade colour with the primary indigo button, three coloured step stones above it.
FORM: Candidate 3 of 7 grounded (pastel timber facades, Gothenburg/Gamla Stan); seed key c3597fc8, assigned; brief-pinned (pastel, lively, friendly shapes, not cartoony, not elegant) translated into this world.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
