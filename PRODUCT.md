# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build step; hosted on GitHub Pages. Progress lives in localStorage.

## Users

A self-learning adult, English as first language, studying Swedish verbs in short daily sessions on phone or laptop.

## Product Purpose

Verbträning teaches Swedish verbs: learn five at a time with four example sentences (infinitiv, presens, preteritum, supinum), then lock them in with spaced-repetition flashcards. Includes particle verbs (grouped by stem verb) and an irregular-verb conjugation drill. Success is a steady learn → practice → review habit.

## Positioning

Guided rather than a flat flashcard deck: "My progress" always names the next step. Content is organised by frequency tiers and the four tenses a Swedish learner must internalise.

## Capabilities and Constraints

151 verbs, 130 particle verbs, 68 irregular verbs; flashcard directions A (EN→SV), B (SV→EN), C (irregular conjugations); SM-2-style scheduling; Swedish text-to-speech via browser. Layout may change in a redesign, behaviour and content stay.

## Evidence on Hand

All content is in data/*.js. No logo, mascot, or brand assets exist yet; none should be fabricated beyond decoration.

## Product Principles

- Always show the next step; never leave the learner choosing blindly.
- Short sessions, low friction, forgiving of mistakes.
- Swedish forms are the hero of every card; everything else supports them.
